# Module 08 — Quality Management System (ISO 9001:2015)

**Depends on:** 00, 04, 07. **Blocks:** nothing.
**Definition of done:** an external auditor can be shown objective evidence for every clause of
ISO 9001:2015 from within the platform, without anyone assembling a folder the night before.

> **Framing.** AIES is **not yet certified** and wants its processes aligned so that
> certification is achievable. This module does not certify anything — certification requires a
> registrar audit and real management commitment. What the platform can do is make conformity the natural by-product of doing the
> work, rather than a parallel paperwork exercise. Build it that way: almost every clause below
> is satisfied by data that modules 01–07 already produce.

## 2. NCR and CAPA

```prisma
model NonConformity {
  id, number, source          // internal_audit | customer_complaint | supplier | field_work | inspection | process | management_review
  raisedById, raisedAt, detectedAt
  entityType?, entityId?      // the ticket, QA inspection, goods receipt, or project it relates to
  accountId?, supplierId?
  title, description, evidenceFileIds String[]
  severity                    // minor | major | critical
  category                    // product | service | process | documentation | safety | delivery
  immediateAction?, containmentAt?, containedById?
  disposition?                // rework | repair | regrade | return | scrap | accept_by_concession
  dispositionApprovedById?    // concession requires authority — clause 8.7
  status                      // open | investigating | action_planned | action_in_progress | verification | closed | cancelled
  ownerId, dueAt?, closedAt?
  costImpact?                 // cost of poor quality
}

model CorrectiveAction {
  id, number, ncrId?, title, problemStatement
  rootCauseMethod             // 5_why | fishbone | 8d | other
  rootCauseAnalysis Json      // structured 5-why chain or fishbone categories
  actions Json                // [{ description, ownerId, dueAt, status, completedAt }]
  effectivenessCheckDue, effectivenessCheckedAt?, effectivenessResult?, effectivenessNotes?
  preventiveMeasures?, documentsUpdated String[]
  status, ownerId
}
```

- NCRs can be raised from anywhere: a failed checklist item (module 04 auto-raises), a **failed
  QA inspection with major or critical defects** (module 04 §9 auto-raises), an **AIES-caused
  warranty claim** (module 04 §11 auto-raises), rejected goods (module 03 auto-raises), a
  customer complaint, or an audit finding.
- **Rework rounds are the leading indicator.** A ticket that passes QA on its third attempt has
  cost the company three crews' time and produced no NCR unless someone raised one. Report rework
  rounds alongside NCRs, and treat a rising rework rate as a systemic signal even when every
  individual job eventually passed.
- **A CAPA cannot be closed without a recorded effectiveness check performed after a defined
  interval.** This is the most commonly failed part of ISO in practice; enforce it in software.
- Trend view: NCRs by category, source, supplier, customer, and month. Repeat NCRs in the same
  category flag a systemic issue.

## 3. Calibration — an outsourced process, not an in-house lab

**Confirmed: AIES does not operate its own accredited laboratory.** Calibration is subcontracted
to an ISO/IEC 17025 accredited laboratory (e.g. Philippine Geoanalytics Calibration and
Measurement Laboratory Corporation).

That changes this section fundamentally. Under ISO 9001 this is **clause 8.4 control of
externally provided processes** as much as clause 7.1.5. The company's obligation is not to
perform calibration correctly — it is to choose a competent provider, verify their accreditation
covers the measurement in question, and keep the traceability chain intact through its own hands.

Do not build uncertainty budgets, method validation, or proficiency testing. Build custody,
scheduling, and evidence.

### 3.1 Two populations of instruments

| | **AIES-owned** | **Customer-owned** |
|---|---|---|
| Why calibrated | To keep AIES's own measurements valid | Because the customer bought calibration as a service |
| Cost | AIES overhead | Billable, quoted through modules 02/03 |
| Custody risk | Internal | **Customer property — clause 8.5.3.** Loss or damage raises an NCR automatically. |
| Drives | Blocking of overdue instruments on material requests | A ticket, a project, and a billing milestone |

Both use the same job record; the difference is ownership and who pays.

```prisma
model CalibrationAsset {
  id, assetTag, name, manufacturer, modelNumber, serialNumber
  ownership          // aies | customer
  accountId?, siteId?               // when customer-owned
  type, rangeSpec, accuracySpec, custodianId?, currentLocation
  status             // in_office | issued_to_ticket | at_laboratory | in_transit | returned | lost | decommissioned
  calibrationIntervalMonths, lastCalibratedAt?, nextCalibrationDue?
  lastCertificateFileId?, isReferenceStandard Boolean
}

model CalibrationJob {
  id, number                        // CAL-{YY}-{####}
  assetIds String[], laboratoryId
  ticketId?, salesOrderId?          // when sold as a service
  ownership          // aies | customer
  requestedAt, sentAt?, expectedReturnAt?, receivedBackAt?, returnedToCustomerAt?
  outboundWaybillRef?, inboundWaybillRef?
  conditionOnDispatch Json, conditionOnReturn Json, photoFileIds String[]
  status             // requested | dispatched | at_laboratory | completed | received | returned_to_customer | cancelled
  result             // in_tolerance | out_of_tolerance | adjusted | unserviceable
  certificateFileId?, certificateNumber?, calibratedAt?, nextDueAt?
  traceableTo, cost?, notes
}

model Laboratory {
  id, name, address, contactName?, email?, phone?
  accreditationBody, accreditationNumber, accreditationScope
  accreditationCertificateFileId, accreditationExpiryAt
  typicalTurnaroundDays, isApproved Boolean, lastEvaluatedAt?, rating?
}
```

### 3.2 What the platform must actually do

- **Approved laboratory register.** The lab's accreditation certificate, number, **scope**, and
  expiry on file. Two checks that matter and are usually skipped:
  1. **Expiry.** Dispatching instruments to a laboratory whose accreditation lapsed invalidates
     the certificates. Block dispatch and warn 90 days ahead.
  2. **Scope.** Accreditation covers specific measurement types and ranges. A lab accredited for
     pressure is not thereby accredited for flow. Record the scope and flag when an instrument's
     type falls outside it — this is the failure an auditor will find, and it is invisible
     without a register.
- **Custody chain.** Dispatch and return with waybill references, condition photos both ways, and
  a live location per instrument. For customer-owned instruments this is clause 8.5.3 evidence
  and, more practically, it is what settles the argument when a customer says their transmitter
  came back scratched.
- **Turnaround tracking.** Expected vs actual return, feeding the lab's supplier evaluation in
  §5. Late calibration returns cascade into missed customer commitments.
- **Certificate filing.** Certificates land in the DMS (module 07) linked to the instrument's
  serial number, so they can be reissued to a customer years later without anyone opening a
  filing cabinet.
- **Due-date dashboard.** Instruments due within 60 days; overdue AIES instruments are **blocked
  from issue on a material request** (module 04 §7). An overdue reference standard invalidates
  every measurement taken with it.
- **Out-of-tolerance impact assessment (clause 7.1.5.2).** When a returned certificate says
  out-of-tolerance, the system lists every ticket that used that instrument since its last valid
  calibration, and the customers and equipment affected — because module 04 records instrument
  issue per ticket and T&C records which instruments took which readings. Produce the list, name
  the affected customers, and require a documented decision on whether to notify them. This is
  one query and it is the single most valuable thing in this module.
- **Calibration sold as a service** flows through the normal path: quotation → PO → ticket →
  collect instruments from the customer → dispatch to lab → receive → return to customer →
  service report → billing. The subcontracted cost is a project cost like any other, so margin on
  calibration work is visible.

### 3.3 What not to build

No uncertainty calculations, no method validation records, no proficiency-testing schedule, no
environmental condition logging. Those are the accredited laboratory's obligations under 17025,
not AIES's under 9001. If AIES ever pursues its own accreditation, that is a new module and a
different conversation.

## 4. Competence and training (clause 7.2)

```prisma
model Competency        { id, name, category, description, requiresCertification, validityMonths? }
model UserCompetency    { id, userId, competencyId, level, achievedAt, expiresAt?, evidenceFileIds String[], assessedById }
model TrainingRecord    { id, userId, title, provider, type, completedAt, expiresAt?, certificateFileId, cost?, effectivenessAssessment? }
model CompetencyRequirement { id, roleId?, jobType?, competencyId, isMandatory }
```

- **Competence matrix** view: users × competencies, colour-coded current / expiring / expired.
- Feeds module 04's skills-based scheduling **and its mobilization readiness check**: an expired
  certification removes the technician from eligible assignment, and a crew containing an
  uncertified member fails the readiness check before mobilization rather than after. Documented
  override path in both cases.
- Expiry notifications at 90, 60, and 30 days to the individual and their manager.

## 5. Supplier evaluation (clause 8.4)

```prisma
model SupplierEvaluation {
  id, supplierId, period, evaluatedById, evaluatedAt
  criteria Json      // quality, delivery, price, responsiveness, documentation — scored
  overallScore, rating   // approved | conditional | probation | disqualified
  ncrCount, onTimeDeliveryPct, notes, nextEvaluationDue
}
```

- Metrics auto-computed from real data: on-time delivery from supplier PO expected vs actual
  arrival (module 03), NCR count from this module, incoming inspection rejection rate.
- Approved supplier list with expiry; raising a supplier PO to a disqualified supplier is blocked,
  and to a `conditional` supplier requires an approval step.

## 6. Internal audit and management review

```prisma
model AuditProgramme { id, year, scope, approvedById, plannedAudits Json }
model InternalAudit  { id, number, programmeId, scope, clauses String[], auditorIds String[], auditeeIds String[], plannedAt, conductedAt?, status, checklist Json, findings Json, reportFileId? }
model ManagementReview {
  id, number, meetingDate, attendeeIds String[]
  inputs Json     // clause 9.3.2 agenda: audit results, customer feedback, process performance, NCR/CAPA status, objectives, supplier performance, resources, risks
  outputs Json    // clause 9.3.3: improvement decisions, QMS changes, resource needs
  actionItems Json, minutesFileId, approvedById
}
```

- Audit findings create NCRs automatically, inheriting the audit reference.
- The management review agenda is **pre-populated from live platform data**: this quarter's
  on-time delivery, NCR trend, CAPA closure rate, customer complaints, objective attainment,
  supplier scores. The meeting stops being a data-gathering exercise and becomes a decision
  meeting. Action items become tasks (module 06).

## 7. Registers

- **Risk and opportunity register** (clause 6.1): risk description, process affected, likelihood,
  impact, score, treatment, owner, review date. Link risks to the processes they threaten.
- **Quality objectives** (clause 6.2): objective, metric, target, baseline, owner, review period —
  each bound to a KPI from module 09 so attainment is measured, not asserted.
- **Customer feedback and complaints** (clause 9.1.2): complaints route to NCR; satisfaction
  surveys triggered on `project.closed` with a simple 3-question form and NPS.
- **Customer property register** (clause 8.5.3): customer equipment held at AIES for repair or
  calibration — received date, condition on receipt with photos, location, return date. Loss or
  damage of customer property raises an NCR automatically.

## 8. A note on clause 8.3 (design and development)

Many industrial suppliers exclude 8.3 on the basis that they supply and install to
manufacturer specification and do not design. AIES's site describes "supply, installation, and
**design** of electrical, mechanical, and instrumentation systems", which suggests 8.3 applies.

Implement a light design control record (design inputs, review, verification, validation,
outputs, changes) attachable to a project, **but flag this to the user as a decision to confirm
with their ISO consultant** — the scope of 8.3 applicability is a certification-scope question,
not a software question.

## 12. Tests

- CAPA cannot close without a completed effectiveness check dated after the interval.
- Out-of-tolerance instrument correctly lists all affected tickets (via material request issue)
  and all T&C records that named it, since last valid calibration.
- QA failure with a critical defect raises exactly one NCR; a `pass_with_observations` raises
  none.
- An AIES-caused warranty claim raises an NCR; a customer-misuse warranty claim does not.
- Expired competency removes the technician from eligible assignment; override is logged.
- Disqualified supplier blocks PO creation.
- Management review inputs pull correct live figures for a fixed test dataset.
